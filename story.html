Our story

The people behind Tasiaeafe.

Tipa Wellness Supplements brews Tasiaeafe by hand in Petesa Tai, Pago Pago, and sells it by the gallon to families across Tutuila. This is the house, the recipe, and the record behind every jug.

The ledger

Entry by entry.

A brew house keeps a book. Here's ours — where Tasiaeafe comes from, how it reaches you, and what stands behind it.

The house

year to confirm

A kitchen in Petesa Tai, not a factory.

Everything starts in a house in Petesa Tai, Pago Pago. The roots and spices are simmered in small pots — never more than a few gallons at a time — and when a batch runs out, the next one goes on the stove. There is no production line to keep running and no warehouse to empty.

The recipe

year to confirm

Nine ingredients, and no substitutions.

Turmeric, ginger, garlic, cinnamon, cayenne pepper, olive oil, honey, lemon and purified water. That is the whole list, printed on the side of every gallon exactly as it goes into the pot.

The jugs

year to confirm

Bottled by the gallon, stamped by hand.

The finished brew is pasteurized, poured into gallon jugs, sealed and moved straight into the chiller. Every jug is marked by hand with its batch date and best-by date before it leaves the house, so any bottle can be traced back to the pot it came from.

The expos

first expo to confirm

Meeting Tutuila face to face.

Tasiaeafe is sold the way it's made — in person. The table has stood at the Alofau SDA Health Expo and in the gym at Fagaitua High School, where people can taste a cup, ask what's in it, and carry a gallon home the same day.

The record

permit date to confirm

Inspected, tested, permitted.

The American Samoa Government Department of Health inspected Tasiaeafe and the way it's brewed, passed it, and issued the permit every batch is made and sold under. The Scientific Research Organisation of Samoa tested the contents — herbs included — and verified them safe and good for consumption.
